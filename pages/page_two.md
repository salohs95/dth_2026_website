
# Disadvantages of open source software

The open-source approach also comes with challenges. These include financial sustainability, the role of corporations and the balance between openness and commercial interests.

## The economics of open source

<div class="articleLayout">

<div class="articleText">

The problem of open-source is that programmers still need an income to survive, many of the open-source developers often work for large corporations, companies financially support open-source project (Curto-Millet & Jiménez, 2022).

There is also a contradiction of the open-source movement, as it criticises copyright, but still depend on copyright law to protect its systems, without copyright, large corporations could take the shared code and turn it into proprietary products (Berry, 2008).

## The contradiction of openness

Another layer is that companies might go open-source because it gives them advantages such as low costs due to organisations sharing maintenance and development contributions, not necessarily because they believe in openness. Thus, resulting in selective openness, keeping some of the valuable code closed. Which begs the question if something can be genuinely open when companies still decide which part of their software they open and which part they keep closed? (Curto-Millet & Jiménez, 2022).

## The challenge of maintenance

Another point is that open-source project still needs continuous maintenance, funding and labour. People may contribute to open-source projects because they believe in the project, but they could spend considerable amount of time developing the software, and a company uses that software in a profitable product, the volunteer does not get financial reward but receives recognition (Linåker & Regnell, 2020).
