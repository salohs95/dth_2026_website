
# Open source software in my major

## Introducing Penpot

<div class="articleLayout">

<div class="articleText">

I chose Penpot for an open-source project that could be used in my major. As I am a Digital Design major, Penpot lets designers create interfaces, prototypes and design systems, and collaborate with developers (Penpot, n.d.).

Penpot is an alternative to Figma, and Figma we have used a lot in our major for prototypes. Penpot is a web-based UI/UX and visual design platform that lets designers' prototypes and digital interfaces using native web standards (Penpot, n.d.-b).

Penpot was created by Kaleidos, a Spanish tech company, which was founded in 2011 (Kaleidos, n.d.).

## How Penpot could support my work

Penpot would in my major help me with creating a high-fidelity prototype of my projects. It creates interactive design solutions. For example, it could help me create a website based on user needs, to be tested and evaluated before building the website.

## Funding and sustainability

Penpot is funded primarily by Decibel Partners and Athos Capital, two venture capital firms. Decibel Partners is its main investor, having led both the $8 million Series A round in 2022 (Novet, 2022), and the additional $12 million funding round in 2023 (Lunden, 2023).

## My verdict

For my thesis I could see myself using Penpot. Though as I know Figma better and is what we have used in our major I could see myself using that instead. If I were focused in Open-source theme in my thesis, I would use Penpot.
